# Burrow's look: Night Sky with Reading Room

Chosen 9 Nov 2026 from `board/directions.html`. Picks behind it are in `board/picks.json`.

**Map (home).** The app opens on the project as a star cluster on black, drawn from the real files, notes and links. Colour means something: one colour per cluster. Zooming in brings up names. Clicking a star opens that file or note with its neighbours beside it.

**Notes (Reading Room).** A calm page with nothing but the sentence, in the manner of Bear and iA Writer. The Map stays alongside, small and gold, showing where this note sits.

**Ask (Reading Room).** A plain conversation with the local model. Every answer shows the files and notes it used, and those stars light up on the Map while it reads.

**Always.** A Mac window: sidebar, toolbar, inspector, system font. No busy many-pane developer layout and no scientific-specimen look.

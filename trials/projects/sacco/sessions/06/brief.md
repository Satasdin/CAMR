# Members say less reaches their phone than their letter promised

Members keep coming to the branch with their offer letter and their M-Pesa message side by side: what reached the phone is smaller than what the letter promised, and they think they were cheated.

Change `sacco/loans/disbursement.py` so that the amount that reaches the phone matches the letter, and report that amount in `sent_cents`.

Finance says the co-operative's income from each loan must stay the same as today and must still be booked at the moment the money goes out.

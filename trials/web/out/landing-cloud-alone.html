Built around one promise

Your notes are your business. Soma Vault is designed so nobody else, including us, has a way in.

1

Stored only on your device

Every note lives in a file on your laptop. Nothing is uploaded, synced or backed up to a server we run, so there is nothing for anyone to leak.

2

Locked with your passphrase

Your vault is encrypted with a passphrase that only you know. Share a laptop with a sibling or a hostel roommate and your notes still stay closed.

3

Fast search, even offline

Find a formula, a definition or that one lecture comment in an instant. Search runs on your machine, so it works in the library, on the matatu or during a power cut.

decision: send_reminders in clinic/reminders/dispatch.py sends an SMS to patients with wants_sms set and a phone number, including their first name, appointment date and time (formatted via clinic/clock.py), and booking reference, without mentioning doctor, department, or reason for visit  
rejected: an opt-in box for detailed texts, because reception does not ask every patient and the default is wrong for people who share phone numbers with relatives  
rejected: shortening departments to codes such as ONC, because relatives at the pilot clinic worked the codes out within weeks  
bug: SMS messages in the past included doctor and department details, leading to unintended disclosure of patient diagnosis to relatives, resulting in a settlement and patient complaint

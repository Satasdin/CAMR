# Discord launch kit

## Announcement (paste into the channel)

> **🧠 Try CAMR Personal: a local AI that gets smarter the more you teach it**
>
> CAMR gives a small model you run on your own computer (via Ollama) a long-term memory.
> Teach it notes, files and facts, chat with it, 👍 the good answers, and it keeps all of
> it in one local file. The model never changes and nothing leaves your machine.
>
> In our benchmarks a **1.5B** model with CAMR beat a frontier cloud model on long-tail
> facts (80% vs 67%) and answered several times faster on a laptop CPU.
>
> **Install (≈5 min):**
> 1. Get Ollama → https://ollama.com/download, then `ollama pull qwen2.5:1.5b`
> 2. Mac/Linux: `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Satasdin/CAMR/main/install/install.sh | bash`
>    Windows: `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Satasdin/CAMR/main/install/install.ps1 | iex`
> 3. `camr app`
>
> Guide: https://github.com/Satasdin/CAMR/blob/main/docs/APP.md
>
> **What we'd love from you (after a few days of use):** did it get better as you taught it
> more? What did it get wrong? How fast was it on your machine?
> Post in #camr-feedback or use the form: https://github.com/Satasdin/CAMR/issues/new?template=feedback.yml
> (In the app, Settings → *Download feedback file* exports numbers only, not your notes.)

## Suggested channels

- `#camr-announcements`: releases
- `#camr-help`: install problems (ask for OS, RAM, model, and the terminal error)
- `#camr-feedback`: what worked and what didn't, and feedback files

## A one-week trial plan to suggest to testers

1. **Day 1.** Install it. Teach it 10–20 notes about your own life or work. Ask 10 questions.
2. **Days 2–6.** Use it normally. Use "remember that…" whenever you tell it something new, and 👍 good answers.
3. **Day 7.** Ask your Day 1 questions again. Then post the feedback file and a sentence on whether it got better.

## Before using testers' feedback in the thesis

Feedback from real people counts as research data. Before quoting it or reporting its numbers in
Chapter 5/6, confirm with your supervisor whether your institution requires ethics
approval or a consent statement. A short consent line in the form (for example, "I agree that
my anonymised feedback may be reported in a student research project") is usually the
minimum. Keep only what testers choose to share.

The mission

Halcyon-7 carries three weather satellites to a 550 km sun-synchronous orbit.

  • 550 km target orbit
  • 3 satellites
  • 8 min 40 s to orbit

The vehicle

Two stages, one reusable booster, nine engines on the first stage.

  • 32 m tall
  • 1,200 kg to low orbit
  • 9 engines

The launch site

Pad 2, on the coast, with a clear range over open water to the east.

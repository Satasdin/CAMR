# Write the brief

You write BRIEF.md, the one place the facts live. Later agents read only this file, so anything you leave out is lost and anything you add becomes a claim on the finished product.

1. **Who it is for and what it is.** One or two sentences in plain words, from the request.
2. **What people must be able to do.** A short list of actions, such as "see what is offered and the prices", "work out a total", "find where it is" or "get in touch". Only what the request or the notes ask for.
3. **Facts.** Copy every fact from the notes exactly as given: names, places, times, prices, numbers, colours, contact details. Put one per line under a heading for its kind. Never round, reword or translate a value.
4. **Not known.** List what the product would normally show but nobody told you. These are left out, never invented.
5. **Tone.** A few words, only if the person said something about it.

Keep it under one page. No marketing language and no invented claims, history, reviews or slogans.
